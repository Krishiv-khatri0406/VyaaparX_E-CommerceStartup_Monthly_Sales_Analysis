# 📊 VyaaparX — E-Commerce Sales & Logistics Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

**VyaaparX** is a fictionalized e-commerce startup created for this portfolio project. This project uses a simulated e-commerce dataset to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, and order fulfilment.

The primary objective of the project is to demonstrate practical **Microsoft Excel data-analysis skills** by transforming raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through formulas, PivotTables, charts, KPIs, slicers, and an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Although VyaaparX generates substantial sales and surplus, the analysis reveals a significant **order-fulfilment backlog**.

Approximately **69.5% of orders remain pending**, with pending orders representing approximately **78.86% of total net sales value**.

This creates an important business question:

> **Is VyaaparX generating demand faster than it can fulfil customer orders?**

The project therefore focuses not only on financial performance but also on identifying the scale of the company's potential logistics and fulfilment challenge.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

* **Total Net Sales:** ₹21.33 Million
* **Pending Order Value:** ₹16.82 Million
* **Delivered Order Value:** ₹4.51 Million
* **Pending Order Rate:** 69.5%
* **Pending Sales Value:** 78.86% of total net sales
* The company demonstrates strong sales generation and surplus despite a substantial fulfilment backlog.
* Product and category-level analysis allows high-performing products to be identified through interactive dashboard filters.
* The high proportion of pending orders indicates a potential operational bottleneck requiring further investigation.

> **Note:** The dataset confirms the scale of the fulfilment backlog but does not contain sufficient operational information to establish its exact root cause. Inventory, warehouse capacity, processing times, logistics partners, and geographical delivery data would be required for detailed root-cause analysis.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, VyaaparX should investigate:

1. **Fulfilment Capacity** — Determine whether logistics capacity is sufficient to handle current demand.
2. **Inventory Availability** — Investigate whether stock shortages are contributing to pending orders.
3. **Order Processing** — Analyse the time taken between order placement and dispatch.
4. **Geographical Bottlenecks** — Identify regions with unusually high pending-order concentrations.
5. **Logistics Partners** — Evaluate the performance and capacity of third-party delivery providers.
6. **Pending Order Prioritization** — Develop a system to prioritize older and high-value pending orders.

Additional operational data would be required to determine the exact root cause of the fulfilment problem.
